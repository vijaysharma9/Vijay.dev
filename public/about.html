My Story

From Freelancer to
Full-Stack Partner

I started writing code at 19 with one goal: build things that matter. Over 8 years I've worked with startups, SaaS companies, and enterprise teams across India, the UK, US, and Australia — delivering everything from MVP prototypes to production-grade platforms.

What sets me apart isn't just the code I write — it's how I think about your business. I ask the hard questions before writing a single line. I've saved clients months of rework by catching architectural mistakes early.

Today I run HireDeveloperShop — a lean, senior-only consultancy that gives you agency-quality output at freelance rates. No juniors, no middlemen, no fluff.
